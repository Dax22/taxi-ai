AI & SMART JOURNEY TOOLS

A little intelligence.
More confidence.

Helpful guidance from pickup to arrival. Explore AI-assisted vehicle checks and smart journey tools, with opt-in safety tools in testing.

  • AI-assisted vehicle checks

    When enabled, compare a car photo with your assigned vehicle’s details. Keep checking the car and plate yourself before boarding.

  • Kemmy journey guidance

    See driver and vehicle details, pickup reminders and journey updates together, with arrival estimates when location data is available.

  • Fare guidance. Your decision.

    Start with a route-based suggested fare, then make an offer. You and your driver agree the final price.

  • Possible-crash alerts Experimental

    Opt-in motion and speed checks can start a 30-second alert countdown while the safety screen is open. Contact delivery requires a configured provider; emergency escalation requires a connected partner.

  • Panic alerts & emergency sharing Experimental

    Start a panic alert manually or enable loud-distress checks. With consent and a configured provider, alerts share passenger and driver details and available location. Sound-level checks cannot reliably identify screams.

  • Location risk warnings Preview

    Flag a location for review. Approved, time-limited reports can appear near your saved route and shared position. A missing warning does not mean an area is safe.
