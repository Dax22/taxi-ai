MY STORE

Your kitchen. Your community.

Manage your menu and incoming orders from the same Taxi Ai account.

Create your store

Use fictional details in this preview. A staff member must review the store before it can open.

Kitchen details

Food vendors and home kitchens display only their name, town or area and state; restaurants display their full business address. When an order is ready, share a private collection point with the assigned courier or pickup customer.

Local towns or areas you deliver to

Start with your kitchen’s town. Add only local areas you can serve; this does not enable nationwide food delivery.

Private courier pickup location

At your kitchen, choose “Use my current location” to save a private pickup location for nearby courier matching. It is visible only to you and authorised staff, and is not shown on your public profile. You can save a draft or offer customer pickup without it. Courier delivery in a new town needs a pickup location before the kitchen can open for delivery and accept delivery orders. Changes require a new staff review.

Changing the kitchen type, name, cuisine, pickup address or customer pickup setting requires a new review. Closing pauses new orders; existing orders still need fulfillment.

Your menu

Add a menu item

Use 0 when sold out. Restaurants may leave this blank for unlimited portions. Saving starts a new batch count; cancellations from an older batch will not increase it.

Photograph the actual food you sell, with the correct portion and included sides. Use images you own or have permission to use. Avoid people, private addresses and personal details. JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 8 MiB; photos are resized and location metadata is removed. Photos are optional and reviewed before customers see them.

Kitchen images

Add optional branding after saving your store. JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 8 MiB. Location metadata is removed. Public images need staff approval.

Kitchen orders

Accept the order, start preparation, then mark it ready. For delivery, share the kitchen code with your assigned courier at handover. For customer pickup, enter the customer’s code when you hand them their food.
